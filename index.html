HyperPieces History

HyperPieces is a collection of 2,222 characters living on Hyperliquid. No promises. No roadmap theater. Just art worth collecting.

The project was born loud, sold out fast, and wow'd everyone at reveal. Then the founder drifted off, the way founders sometimes do, leaving a beautiful collection behind. But it was never abandoned. The community stayed. Not for an airdrop, not for a pump, but out of genuine love for the art — holding the line and building when inspired, treating HyperPieces like the fine art collection it deserves to be. This isn't a startup chasing utility. It's an IP with a pulse, and a community that vibes with the aesthetic.
